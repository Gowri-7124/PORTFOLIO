Retail data pipeline (medallion architecture)

Nov 2026

PySpark, Spark standalone cluster, Docker Compose, Parquet, Power BI

An end-to-end PySpark pipeline that cleans 1 million transaction records through bronze, silver and gold layers.

  • Wrote a Python generator that injects realistic defects at scale: repeated transaction IDs, about 50% invalid ages, about 33% negative prices and out-of-range discounts, about 21% ship dates before order dates, and mixed gender codes. Over 99% of raw rows carry at least one defect for the silver layer to fix.
  • Built three gold tables (daily sales metrics, city revenue and product-category performance) and visualised them in a Power BI dashboard.
  • Containerised a Spark cluster with one master and two workers using Docker Compose, so the full pipeline runs the same way on any machine.
